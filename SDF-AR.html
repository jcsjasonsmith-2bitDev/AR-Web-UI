SDF AR interface

HandMap's signed-distance hand-mapping engine, driving a spatial AR interface. Your silhouette becomes the UI surface — the field is rendered as a hologram over the live feed, the 21-node rig aims the reticle, and closing your hand is the click.

Camera + file access need a secure context (https or localhost) and permission. Inside a sandboxed preview they may be blocked — the synthetic demo runs fully offline and drives every gesture.
Tip: keys 1 / 2 / 3 cycle the bundled hand feed · M monitor · H hologram · G gyro · R recenter · S capture · D deploy · C render mode · zero dependencies, no model downloads — all inference runs locally in canvas. Original engine: hand-map-engine.html · spatial shell: ARUI 02.
